# DermaAI

## Project Title

**DermaAI – Explainable Multimodal AI for Skin Disease Diagnosis and Intelligent Decision Support**

---

# Project Overview

DermaAI is an AI-powered healthcare application designed to assist in the early detection of skin diseases using deep learning and multimodal artificial intelligence. The system analyzes skin lesion images along with patient symptoms, demographic information, and medical history to provide accurate disease prediction and intelligent decision support.

The project combines computer vision, natural language processing, and structured clinical data analysis to improve diagnostic accuracy. Explainable AI techniques such as Grad-CAM and SHAP are incorporated to make the model's predictions transparent and understandable.

The final system will be deployed as a Streamlit web application where users can upload images, enter clinical information, and receive AI-assisted diagnostic results.

---

# Problem Statement

Skin diseases affect millions of people worldwide, but timely diagnosis is often limited by the availability of dermatologists. Traditional AI systems mainly rely on image-based diagnosis and ignore valuable clinical information such as symptoms, age, gender, and medical history.

DermaAI addresses this limitation by developing an explainable multimodal AI system that combines multiple sources of patient information to improve prediction accuracy while providing transparent and trustworthy results.
